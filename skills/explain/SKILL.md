Teach the 20% of the topic that covers 80% of the decisions he will face. In chat, read-only, never blocking the work.
1. **What it is:** one paragraph, no jargon; a physical-world analogy if one fits.
2. **Why it matters to the business:** users, money, client trust or risk. If none, say so ("plumbing: it matters only because X breaks without it").
3. **The one concept worth really understanding:** name the jargon term so he recognizes it next time, then translate it at once.
4. **How to judge it next time:** what to ask or look for when it shows up in a future plan or review. This is the payoff; be concrete.
Rules: use the real example from this work, not a textbook one. No code dumps; at most a 2-3 line snippet with every line translated. If the leftover 20% could bite, name it in one line. English by default; in Hebrew when he asks or writes in Hebrew, technical terms kept in English with a translation, laid out per hebrew-text.
