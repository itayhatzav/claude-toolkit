---
name: hebrew-text
description: Write Hebrew briefs, pings or copy for Itay or Roee without scrambling mixed Hebrew and English. Use whenever any Hebrew text is written.
---
Mixed Hebrew and English scrambles reading order, and Itay cannot read it.
- Never put an English word, path, command, URL or number-with-unit in the middle of a Hebrew sentence.
- Write the Hebrew sentence whole, then put the English term in parentheses at the END of the sentence, or on its own line, or in a code block.
- A Hebrew line must not start with a Latin word or code, including after a list marker or bold mark. Write Hebrew first, code in parentheses at the end.
- Lists: one item per line, the English part last. Tables and code blocks are exempt.
- Prefer a Hebrew word with the English term once in parentheses on first use.
- Short and plain. Lead with the outcome or the ask.
